An Imprint is a statement that describes where, by whom, and when the resource was published, produced, distributed and/or manufactured. Prioritize publication information over production, distribution or manufacturing information whenever possible.

## 030100. Place of publisher, producer, distributor, and/or manufacturer (repeatable)

**Definition:** The name of the place associated with the publisher, producer, distributor, and/or manufacturer.

- Source: [1908 ALA rules](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=mdp.39015033881775&view=1up&seq=65){:target="_blank"} (p. 45, modified); [MRIs 1.4 C3](https://special-cataloguing.com/mris/1){:target="_blank"}

**Rule:** Transcribe the place of publication, production, distribution, and/or manufacture from the source of information. If more than one place appears, transcribe all deemed necessary according to local practice.

In cases where the place name is abbreviated or ambiguous, a fuller or clarifying form may be recorded in square brackets after the transcribed form. 

If no place name is on the resource, but the place is known, supply it in square brackets.

If no place name can be determined, supply in square brackets the phrase “Place of publication not determined”.

- Source: [ALA 1908 rules](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=mdp.39015033881775&view=1up&seq=65){:target="_blank"} (p. 45, modified); [MRIs 1.4 C3](https://special-cataloguing.com/mris/1){:target="_blank"}

**MARC field:** 260 \$a ; 264 \$a

>**EXAMPLES:**  
>  
>**_Place of publication_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a New York  
>  
>260 \#\# \$a Paris  
>  
>**_Multiple publication place names_**   
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Amsterdam ; \$a San Diego, CA  
>  
>**_Abbreviated place with its fuller form_**  
>  
>264 \#2 \$a Ontario, CA \[Canada\]  
>  
>**_Clarifying form of place name_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Lugduni \[Lyon\]  
>  
>264 \#3 \$a Barrow, Alaska \[Utqiagvik, Alaska\]  
>  
>**_Place not on resource but known_**
>  
>264 \#0 \$a \[Tehran\]  
>  
>**_Unknown place_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a \[Place of publication not determined\]

## 030200. Name of publisher, producer, distributor, and/or manufacturer (repeatable)

**Definition:** The name of the person or corporate body responsible for the publication, production, distribution, and/or manufacture of a resource. 

- Source: BIBFRAME “[Agent](https://id.loc.gov/ontologies/bibframe.html#c_Agent){:target="_blank"}”; [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/items/241dfed4-58f2-4c19-be99-56745e600df0){:target="_blank"}, 4.2.1

**Rule:**  Transcribe the name of the publisher, producer, distributor, or manufacturer as it appears on the resource. Prioritize the publisher.

If there is no publisher on the resource, prefer the producer, distributor, or manufacturer as available.

If choosing between more than one publisher, record the publisher name that appears first, the more visually prominent name, or the name that is considered most important to users. 

If deemed important, record multiple publishers, producers, distributors, and/or manufacturers.

If a resource does not bear publisher information but it is known from an outside source, such as the publisher's website or catalog, supply it in brackets and, optionally, add a note for justification. 

If no publisher, producer, distributor, or manufacturer can be identified, supply, in brackets, the phrase “publisher not determined”.

- Source: [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/items/241dfed4-58f2-4c19-be99-56745e600df0){:target="_blank"}, 4.2.1; [ALA 1908 rules](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=mdp.39015033881775&view=1up&seq=65){:target="_blank"} (p. 45\)

**MARC field:** 260 \$b ; 264 \$b

>**EXAMPLES:**  
>  
>**_Single publisher_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Lawrence, Kansas : \$b University of Kansas Press
>  
>260 \#\# \$a London : \$b Macmillan  
>  
>**_Multiple publishers_**  
>  
>260 \#\# \$a \[Reston, Va.?\] : \$b U.S. Dept. of the Interior, U.S. Geological Survey ; \$a Washington, DC : \$b For sale by the U.S. G.P.O., Supt. of Docs., \$c \[1992\]  
>  
>**_Multiple publishers, same place_**  
>
>264 \#1 \$a \[United States?\] : \$b Việt Ecology Press : \$b Văn Học Press  
>  
>**_Publisher name supplied from an external source_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Rockville, Maryland : \$b \[Center for Substance Abuse Prevention\]  
>500 \#\# \$a Name of publisher taken from publisher’s website.  
>  
>**_Publisher and distributor deemed important for discovery_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a New York : \$b Metropolitan Museum of Art   
>  
>264 \#2 \$a New York : \$b Abrams  
>  
>**_Publisher not included on the resource_**  
>  
>264 \#3 \$a Lexington, KY : \$b Manufactured by Amazon.com, \$c \[2014\]  
>  
>**_No publisher, producer, distributor, or manufacturer can be identified_**
>  
>264 \#1 \$a New York : \$b \[publisher not determined\]

## 030300. Date of publication, production, distribution, and/or manufacture (repeatable)

**Definition:** The date a resource was published, produced, distributed, or manufactured. Production dates apply only to unpublished resources.

- Source: BIBFRAME “[Date](https://id.loc.gov/ontologies/bibframe.html#p_date){:target="_blank"}” 

**Rule:** Record the publication date as it appears on the resource. 

If a resource does not bear a publication date but the publication date is known or can be inferred from a copyright date, other dates printed on the resource, or an outside source, supply it in square brackets and add a note of justification if needed.

If the cataloger cannot determine a publication date, supply in square brackets either a probable date followed by a question mark, or the phrase “date of publication not determined”.

Add distribution or manufacture dates if considered useful for identification or selection. 

- Source: [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/items/241dfed4-58f2-4c19-be99-56745e600df0){:target="_blank"}, 4.3.8; [LC 1949 rules](https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=mdp.39015030341799&view=1up&seq=32&q1=date+uncertain){:target="_blank"} (p. 20\)

**MARC field:** 260 \$c ; 264 \$c

>**EXAMPLES:**  
>  
>**_Publication date clearly identified on resource_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a New York : \$b Springer, \$c 2024  
>  
>260 \#\# \$a London : \$b Macmillan, \$c 1999  
>  
>**_Distribution date_**  
>
>264 \#2 \$a New York : \$b Distributed Art Publishers, \$c 2006  
>  
>**_Publication date inferred from copyright date_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Chichester : \$b Wiley, \$c \[2023\]  
>  
>**_Probable date with a note on the source_**  
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Whittier, California : \$b Select Nurseries, \$c \[1948?\]  
>588 \#\# \$a Publication date inferred from articles cited on pages 39-40.
>  
>**_Publication date not determined_**
>  
>264 \#1 \$a London : \$b Sheldon Press, \$c \[date of publication not determined\]  
>  
>**_Publication date and distribution date both deemed important for discovery_**
>  
>264 \#1 \$a Hong Kong : \$b Hong Kong University Press, \$c 2009  
>264 \#2 \$a London : \$b Eurospan, \$c 2012

## 030400. Notes on imprint (repeatable)

**Definition:** Other details of publication, manufacturing, distribution, etc., of the resource not previously recorded. May describe imprint variations, irregularities, additional dates, and other nonstandard information.

- Source: [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/handle/20.500.14598/1939){:target="_blank"}, 7.4.1

**Rule:** Record as a note details of variations, irregularities, additional dates, etc., where details are deemed important for identification, selection, or clarification.

**MARC field:** 500

>**EXAMPLE:**   
>  
>500  \#\# \$a Reprint of Detective comics no. 27, which first introduced Batman as a character. Originally published in 1939 by Detective Comics, Inc.

## 030401. Changes to imprint information for multipart monographs (repeatable)

**Definition:** Changes in the place and/or name of the publisher, manufacturer, distributor, etc., that occur after the first or earliest part of a multipart monograph.

- Source: [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/items/241dfed4-58f2-4c19-be99-56745e600df0){:target="_blank"}, 7.4.2 (modified)

**Rule:**  Create additional statements for changes to the publication, manufacture, distribution, etc., of a multipart monograph, if the changes are deemed significant. Record minor variations to publisher, manufacturer, distributor, etc., as a note if deemed important for identification, selection, or clarification.

- Source: [ISBD 2021](https://repository.ifla.org/items/241dfed4-58f2-4c19-be99-56745e600df0){:target="_blank"}, 4.2.13, 7.4.1

**MARC field:** 260 ; 264 ; 500

>**EXAMPLES:**  
>  
>**_Additional imprint statement due to significant changes in publication information_**  
>  
>264 21 \$a Milwaukie, Oregon : \$b Riveredge, \$c 1989-2016.  
>264 31 \$a Salem, Oregon : \$b Ankeny Publishing, \$c 2017-
>
>264 21 \$3 v.10-16 : \$a Seattle : \$b Richards Co., \$c 2009-2012.  
>264 31 \$3 v.17- : \$a Boise, Idaho : \$b Russets Inc., \$c 2012-  
>  
>**_Note for minor variation in publication information_**
>    
>260 \#\# \$a Sŏul T'ŭkpyŏlsi : \$b Tan'guk Taehakkyo Ch'ulp'anbu, \$c 1999-2008.  
>500 \#\# \$a Place of publication changed to Kyŏnggi-do Yongin-si around 2008\.
